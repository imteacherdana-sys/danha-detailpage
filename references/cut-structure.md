# Cut Structure

Choose exactly 10, 12, or 18 cuts. Keep every cut as an independent final image file.

## Coupang Required Order

For Coupang pages, lock these positions:

| Cut | Required Role |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Today departure banner: `오늘 당장 출발` |
| 2 | Review Excel image: 5 vivid review bubbles, 4 USP check points, CTA |
| Last | Product information disclosure |

The main persuasive detail-page flow starts after cut 2 and continues until the bottom disclosure.

## Per-Cut Planning Fields

Each cut plan must include:

- Cut number and name
- Purpose in the purchase journey
- Headline
- Subcopy
- Text to render inside the final image
- Image composition
- Product photo placement
- Facts used and facts needing confirmation
- ASCII wireframe for approval
- Compliance caution, if any
